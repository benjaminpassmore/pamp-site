Delete your data

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (draft).

Your information is yours. This page says how to remove it from Pamp, all of it or just one part.

Delete your account and everything in it

  1. Email support@pamp.quest from the email address you use to log in to Pamp, with the subject "Delete my Pamp account".
  2. We reply to check it's really you, so no one else can delete your account.
  3. We delete your account and everything in it, and email you when it's done. To confirm: how many days this takes at most.

That deletes:

  • your account: your email address and name;
  • your answers, and everything you've added, like your "This week" list;
  • your conversations with your AI manager, and what Pamp remembers about you;
  • the record of how much AI you've used;
  • any platforms you've connected, and the numbers Pamp kept from them.

To confirm: what we must keep for a while by law (for example, payment records, once Pamp has paid plans), and for how long.

Delete just one part

  • What Pamp remembers: read, change or clear it in the app, in Settings.
  • Your answers: change them in Settings, any time.
  • Anything else: email support@pamp.quest and say what you'd like removed.

Disconnect a platform

When you connect a platform like Instagram, Facebook, YouTube or TikTok, Pamp keeps a key that lets it read your numbers, and a daily copy of those numbers for your Dashboard. To confirm: connecting platforms isn't in the app yet.

  • In Pamp: in Settings, choose the platform, then Disconnect. Pamp deletes its key and the numbers it kept from that platform straight away.
  • On the platform: you can also remove Pamp from the platform's own settings, for example the "Apps and websites" page in your Facebook or Instagram settings, or "Third-party apps and services" in your Google Account. Pamp can no longer read anything from there. To confirm: Pamp deletes what it kept when a platform tells us you've removed it.

Questions

Email support@pamp.quest. More on what Pamp keeps and why is in our privacy page.
