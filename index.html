What Pamp does

Talk it through

Ask your manager anything, any time: a release, a gig offer, what to post, how to start earning from your work.

Your week, planned

Up to three goals and the time to reach them, planned with your manager. Every Monday, Pamp suggests three things to start with.

Bigger than a week

An EP, a zine, a series: a project with a date and its own checklist, which your manager keeps in mind.

It knows you

Pick how your manager talks to you, from straight shooter to hype. Pamp keeps short notes about you, in your words, that you can read, change or clear.

Who it's for

Musicians, video makers, photographers, designers, writers, podcasters, performers and anyone else making things. Pamp is for you, the person doing the work. It's not a tool for managers looking after other people.

Good to know

Pamp's advice comes from AI. It can be wrong, so check anything about contracts, money or rights with someone qualified before you act on it.
