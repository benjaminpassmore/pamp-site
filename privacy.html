This page says, in plain words, what Pamp keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: hello@pamp.quest.

Who we are

Pamp is an AI manager for small, independent creatives, run by Pamp Global, LLC. To confirm: the company's address.

What Pamp keeps

  • Your account: your email address and the name you give us. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider; Pamp never sees it.
  • Your answers: what you make, what you want most, where you share your work, your style, and how you'd like your manager to talk to you.
  • What you add: the name you go by, your "This week" list, and how you've arranged your Dashboard.
  • Your conversations with your AI manager.
  • What Pamp remembers: short notes about you, written from your conversations. You can read, change or clear them in Settings.
  • How much AI you use: for each reply, which AI model wrote it, how long it took, how many words (tokens) it used and what it cost us. Never what you or Pamp wrote.
  • When you made each AI request, so we can keep usage fair.
  • Numbers you add yourself: streams, listeners and followers you copy in from Spotify for Artists, Apple Music for Artists or SoundCloud. Only you can see them.
  • If you join the waitlist: your email address. We use it only to invite you to Pamp. To confirm (draft): to stop spam, we also keep a scrambled form of your internet address, deleted the next time someone signs up a day or more later. Ask and we'll take you off the list.

What Pamp doesn't do

  • No ads, and we don't sell your information.
  • No passwords or card numbers in what Pamp remembers: they're taken out, even if they come up in a conversation.
  • No tracking or analytics on this website.

Who helps us run Pamp

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and our database.
  • Google Cloud: runs the Pamp server.
  • Anthropic: the AI that writes your manager's replies. To write a reply it receives your message, the conversation so far, your name and the name you go by, your answers and what Pamp remembers about you. To confirm (draft): also a screenshot you choose to share, to read the numbers from it. Pamp doesn't keep the picture; only the numbers you confirm are saved.
  • To confirm (draft, before YouTube is switched on): YouTube: if you connect your channel, Pamp uses YouTube API Services to read your channel's numbers (subscribers, views, watch time, likes, comments and shares), to show them to you on your Dashboard. They're never shared, and kept for 30 days at most. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can remove Pamp's access in Pamp (My platforms) or at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Expo: delivers updates to the app.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.
  • To confirm (draft, before it's switched on): when you chat, the AI provider also gets a short summary of your planner week, your "To do" list and your numbers, so your manager can see them. Music numbers are labelled as ones you added yourself.
  • To confirm if web search is switched on: the AI may search the web for current facts, and those searches run through Anthropic.
  • To confirm: who hosts our email (hello@ and support@).
